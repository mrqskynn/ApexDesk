# SOP-13: Teams & Zoom Audio, Camera, and Screen Sharing Issues

## Overview
Use this procedure when an employee experiences no microphone audio, no speaker output, a black/missing webcam feed, or broken screen sharing in Microsoft Teams or Zoom.

## Resolution Steps
1. **Check Hardware Mute & Privacy Shutter**:
   - Verify the headset inline mute button is off and the laptop camera privacy shutter is slid open.
2. **Select Correct Audio/Video Device in App Settings**:
   - Open Teams/Zoom **Settings > Devices** (or Audio/Video).
   - Explicitly select the active headset/microphone and speakers instead of "Default" if multiple virtual audio devices exist.
   - Run **Make a Test Call** (Teams) or **Test Speaker & Mic** (Zoom).
3. **Verify OS Privacy Permissions**:
   - Windows: **Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone / Camera** -> ensure desktop apps and Teams/Zoom are allowed.
   - macOS: **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera, Microphone, and Screen Recording** -> toggle Zoom/Teams ON and restart the app (required for screen sharing).
4. **Close Conflicting Apps & Reconnect**: Close other apps that may lock the webcam (another browser tab or conferencing tool), unplug and re-plug USB headsets, and test with the web client (`teams.microsoft.com` or `zoom.us/wc`) if needed.

## Escalation Criteria
- Escalate to **Desktop Support** if the internal camera/mic does not appear in Device Manager / System Information (hardware cable or driver failure).
- Escalate to **AV / Conference Room Support** if the issue occurs inside a conference room Zoom/Teams Room console.
